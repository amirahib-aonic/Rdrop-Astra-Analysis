Current, fuse and thermal boundaries

The later brief specifies 5–7 A normal servo current and 15 A for 30 seconds. The developer chose approximately 15 A trip over guaranteed pulse endurance: MF-RG900 can interrupt that pulse. The redesigned board uses 2 oz copper; it is not an equivalent 1 oz comparison.

Named 3–14S UBEC is appropriate only within its input rating. The Step 1 developer notes report a 20 A APM rating, while the redesign report requests exact model and pulse/thermal evidence. Preserve that distinction; source ratings do not qualify the complete PCB path.

Circuit validation

Needs extensive circuit, inter-circuit and placement validation. Individual IR drivers are a positive addition.

Routing quality

Some straight horizontal routes are good; other routes are awkward and appear influenced by walkaround behavior.

Power distribution

Developer prefers dedicated power planes over very large 4.5 mm power traces, to improve efficiency and reduce loop inductance and noise.

Product integration

Connector placement needs clearer user constraints. A freely chosen outline could permit a more efficient layout.

Schematic readability

The generated schematic requires extensive zooming to read comfortably.

Workflow value

Fabrication exports and the connection list are useful automation outputs. Git integration is a future opportunity.

Component count

69 versus 78 footprints is fewer, but footprint count alone does not establish BOM cost savings.

Overall assessment

Passable and promising as a starting point; thoughtful layout refinement and validation are still needed.
