Your review, distilled.

Paraphrased from your notes; separate from the generated analysis.

Ground clearance

Good reduction of excessive clearance: more return copper and fewer isolated thermal-spoke conditions.

Stitching placement

Useful stitching of wide areas, but placement feels random and intrudes into the serial barcode region.

Power trace widths

Good initiative, but changes occur mid-trace, are abrupt and lack tapering; narrow sections remain. Original 1 mm was considered sufficient for the relatively low 5 V load.

Layout scope

No component-placement or overall routing optimization was attempted.

Overall assessment

Minimal and conservative. Stronger execution would use consistent widths and more intentional via placement; the supplied verification records are useful.
